01
Real models, nothing swapped
Chat comes from NCAIR1/N-ATLaS and transcripts from the four N-ATLaS speech recognition models, on the same GPU backend and gateway your own key would use. A "Mock backend" badge appears if the site is ever pointed at the development mock.


02
Shared, so limited
Everyone here shares one demo key. Limits: 10 requests a minute per network, a daily quota on the key, chats of up to 10 messages and 512 tokens, clips of up to 30 seconds, speech of up to 300 characters. Your visit counts once toward the N-ATLaS 1,000-user licence cap.


03
Build on it
Copy the code next to any response, install @openatlas/sdk, and request your own key. N-ATLaS is for non-commercial use under its Terms of Use.
